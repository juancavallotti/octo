# Shipping policy

Follow these rules when a customer asks about shipping or delivery.

- Standard shipping is 5-7 business days; express is 2 business days.
- We ship to the US and Canada only.
- A tracking number is emailed once the order leaves the warehouse.
- Lost-in-transit claims can be filed after 10 business days.
- We do not ship to PO boxes for express orders.

When you have the answer, respond with the shipping information the customer needs.
